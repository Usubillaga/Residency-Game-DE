### [Sicher] P2 — Pluvicto: discontinuation after more than four weeks stated too broadly

**Finding ID:** `RES-P2-PLUVICTO-DELAY-CAUSE` · **Relevance grade 1 — very relevant.** Incorrectly teaching that treatment discontinuation is mandatory can affect clinical interpretation; adding the adverse-reaction qualifier restores fidelity to the source.

`data/protocols.json`, regimen `pca-lu177-psma-617`, field `cautions`, DE/EN/ES: The learning overview required discontinuation for any delay of more than four weeks. The current [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/pluvicto-epar-product-information_en.pdf), §4.2, PDF page 3, explicitly limits this rule to a **delay due to an adverse reaction**. Without this reason, an administrative delay could incorrectly be taught as requiring permanent discontinuation.

Correction: exactly three caution texts were clarified; dose, interval, approved setting and question contract unchanged.
