Night Shift

Urology · Emergency Department · 22:00 to 07:30

Your first night on call as a urology resident. Five patients, one nurse, an attending on call from home – and at 07:30 a head of department who wants to hear every case.

How to play

  • Every action costs game time. While you are with one patient, the others keep evolving.
  • You can delegate bloods, IV fluids and urine dipsticks to Grace – but she only has two hands.
  • You call the attending from the chart. You need them for real emergencies, not for trivia.
  • At 07:30 the head of department quizzes you on your cases at morning report.
